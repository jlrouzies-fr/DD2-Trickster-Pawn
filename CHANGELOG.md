# Changelog

## 0.2.6

A filler attack for the time no skill is due: the pawn swings its censer where it stands, turned to an enemy 4-14 m away, and a ball of incense smoke flies to that enemy (the game's own thrown-smoke projectile). A hit does a little damage (20 + 5 for each level of the pawn, "dust_damage" sets it) and does not draw the enemy's attention to the pawn. While the decoy is out, the enemy that was hit is fooled for 6 s: it lets go of your pawns and goes for the decoy ("dust_fool": false switches that off; big enemies are not fooled). In a test fight the pawn threw one about every 15 s and two in three hit; "dust": false switches it off. The smoke of this attack is kept small: the thick cloud the game's own censer swing throws is taken out, and the puff at the impact fades within a third of a second. Latching Effigy: the mod now throws the incense itself, at the target's legs. The game's own throw flies over small enemies unless they stand higher than the pawn, which is why 0.2.5 only cast it at those; the pawn casts it at level small enemies again. In testing it latched on goblins level with or below the pawn in about half of the casts (2 in 21 before), and on rogues in 2 casts of 3. Thrown smoke flies less far at a low frame rate (about 7 m at 30 FPS, 13 m at 40); the mod only throws at enemies within that reach.

## 0.2.5

Aromatic Rally every 12 s instead of 25, but only with three quarters of the pawn's stamina or more (a Rally costs about half of it): in solo test fights Rally went from about a quarter to about a third of the battle time and the time spent only keeping distance from about 40% to about 30%. Latching Effigy is now aimed: the rule only takes a target 3-10 m away that the pawn faces (until now it waited for an enemy at 3-6 m in any direction; of 35 casts sampled none latched when the pawn was turned away from its target), and a small enemy only when it stands higher than the pawn (aimed casts latched 8 times in 9 on a higher goblin, 2 times in 21 on a level or lower one: the incense flies over them). On flat ground against small enemies the pawn therefore rarely casts it now, instead of wasting half its stamina; "effigy_small_rise": 0 lifts that. Dragon's Delusion, Delusory Screen and Latching Effigy are not started on low stamina, and a running Delusion is ended when stamina runs low: the pawn could cast itself out of breath. Settings: a value you never changed in config.json now follows the default when a later version changes it (the new file config.defaults.json keeps track); values you changed stay yours.

## 0.2.4

The decoy is summoned and re-summoned only while the party is in battle: until now a decoy that had dispersed was brought back out of combat too (in battle it still returns about 6 s after enemies destroy it; "resummon_seconds" changes that). New: when Aromatic Rally is ready and neither you nor another pawn is within 12 m, the pawn dashes back to you and casts it there; fighting alone with it, Rally went from about 5% to about 24% of the battle time in testing, and the time it only holds its distance from 71% to about half ("regroup": false turns the dash off, "regroup_stop" sets how close it comes). Tested with a hired Fighter in the party: the Fighter keeps its own behaviour. The script, its notes and the test tools are on GitHub (jlrouzies-fr/DD2-Trickster-Pawn).

## 0.2.3

Fixes the fights in which the pawn followed and circled but cast nothing. Its casts were built from a copy of a Warrior attack rule and kept three of its checks: an enemy its weapon cannot hurt was never a valid target (against phantoms it did nothing at all), a target point had to be 0-2 m high, and its own AI state had to be the normal fighting one (while the game had it in its danger state, seen with five or six goblins around a level 1 party, every cast was refused for the whole fight). Its casts no longer carry those checks; they follow the party's battle state instead. 0.2.2 listed this as a limit of disable / enable: that was wrong, three disable / enable cycles in a row were followed by normal fights. Aromatic Rally is now also cast when another party pawn (not only you) is within 12 m, and is no longer started with less than half of its stamina (Rally could drain the pawn until it was winded). Measured for reference: a vanilla Mage pawn strafes for about 60% of a fight too, but casts while doing it; the Trickster pawn casts for about a third of a fight.

## 0.2.2

Hired pawns: when a pawn joins or leaves the party the main pawn is set up again (about 20 s as a plain pawn, then its Trickster rules are back), so its rules never sit on the rules of a vocation a party pawn has; tested with a hired Mage, Thief and Archer, who fought normally. Its censer is now equipped the game's own way: the Equipment menu shows it as equipped (0.2.1 showed its old staff), the save keeps it, and a censer you give it and equip in the game's Equipment menu works and is remembered. A death and Load from Autosave was tested: the pawn is set up again by itself. The crash still seen in testing at the title screen after open-world fights was traced to another mod of the test install (a graphics add-on) and to a game fault that is there without this mod.

## 0.2.1

Fixes the random crashes of 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 (a few seconds after a load, or a second or two into a fight): the script let the game free objects it had just created, and whether that happened inside the same frame decided the crash; fixes two causes of the crash at the title screen after returning from the game, and the failures in a second or later load of the same session: when the game leaves the world its rules are removed and the vanilla AI data the mod borrowed is put back first, the pawn no longer gets an extra Trickster action selector that pawns never have, and nothing looked up in one load is reused in the next; a pawn that became a Trickster with an earlier version loses the two unusable cut skills it was given next to Sweeping Shroud, and a new pawn no longer gets them; its core skills are now remembered in pawns.json; voice lines that really play: the Delusory Screen line of 0.2.0 never started, it is replaced, and Aromatic Rally, Dragon's Delusion and a latched effigy get a line of their own; the setup is no longer started on a pawn the game is still placing (after a long teleport it enabled and reset in a loop); the decoy is no longer re-summoned from the script while the pawn's own summon is still creating it.

## 0.2.0

The rank 4 to 7 skills get rules of their own: Latching Effigy (decoy out, two or more enemies around it, one of them 3-6 m from it on its level; when none is in reach it stops backing off for a few seconds), Fickle Floor (a drop of 4 m or more between it and the nearest enemy), Espial Incense (enemies far from it and from the decoy: it enters the trance and the decoy is sent next to the enemy nearest the Arisen), Scented Alarum (out of battle, every 5 minutes); Dragon's Delusion is ended after 5 s instead of being held until the pawn is exhausted; the pawn speaks vanilla pawn lines when it summons the decoy or casts Sweeping Shroud, Delusory Screen or Scented Alarum (one line per 45 s at most, "voice": false turns it off); its keep-distance stance no longer blocks its casts; a save loaded later in the same session is meant to get the Trickster setup again (not verified, restart the game if it does not); the pawn no longer has a combat rule for a skill it has not equipped; new buttons for the four skills in the REFramework panel.

## 0.1.0

First release (beta): the main pawn takes the Trickster vocation at every load and fights with the skills it has equipped (decoy, beckon, skill casts, 7-12 m stance); it starts with the rank-1 Trickster skills and keeps the skills, core skills, augments and censer you give it (the censer, which the game drops at load, is remembered by the mod); Trickster listed in its Vocation Guild, Status page and skill pages; discipline, rank and skill purchases go through the game's own menus; REFramework panel with enable / disable, censer list and debug mode.
